What everything means

A good contract to take usually shows: Clean no Wide tag, no big ↓ number, not dimmed, and the Window open pill is green.

How the contract moves

Clean
Normal pushes are big enough to reach your first target, and normal dips stay well inside your stop. This is what you want.
Choppy
A normal 1-minute dip eats 60% or more of your stop. You'll likely get shaken out before it pushes, even if your read is right.
Slow
A normal 1-minute push is less than a quarter of your first target. The contract is "dead." Even a clean move in the stock may not get you paid.
↓2.1×
Lopsided. It drops 2.1 times harder than it pushes. Anything 1.5× or more shows up. Higher means up moves pay less than down moves cost.

Tracked contract panel

Big number
How many contracts to buy right now. It updates on its own as the price moves.
Exit plan
With scaling on: sell part at your first target, then the next scale is set at the stock's next key level, and runners aim for the level after that. Choppy or slow contracts take more off sooner. With scaling off: sell everything at your first target.
Key levels
Today's high and low, yesterday's high, low and close, VWAP, today's open, and round numbers. The ≈ price is roughly what the contract would be worth if the stock gets there.
1:1 exit
The price that makes the same % as your stop. It only shows when your first target is different from your stop.
Swing chart
Green bar is a normal 1-minute push, red bar is a normal 1-minute drop. The dashed lines are your target and stop.
Price ladder
Contract count and exits at each nearby price. The highlighted row is the current price.
Still 3 until…
The price where your contract count changes by one.

What to scan

Uses the nearest expiration for each ticker. Spread is ask minus bid, as a % of the mid price.

Sizing & exits

On: sell part at your first target, then the tool picks the next scale from the stock's key levels and how the contract is moving. The backup target is used when no key level is close. Off: sell everything at your first target.

Tradier account

  1. Sign in at dash.tradier.com. No account yet? Open a free one at tradier.com.
  2. Open Settings → API Access, or go straight to dash.tradier.com/settings/api.
  3. Copy the Access Token. Use the production token with Live account below, or the sandbox token with Sandbox.
  4. Paste it in the box above and press Connect.

If prices look delayed, check the market data settings on your Tradier account. Never share your token. Anyone who has it can trade your account.

The token stays in your browser and only goes to Tradier. It can also place orders, so only paste it into a copy of this tool you trust.

Alerts

Fires when your first target hits, the stock reaches the next scale level, your stop is hit, or the stock tags a key level. Alerts show on this page; keep the tab open.

Share your setup

Makes a link that opens this tool with your scanner, target, stop, exit and display settings already filled in. Position size, prop firm numbers and your Tradier token are never included. People can still change anything after.

The link works on your hosted copy of the tool. When someone opens it, the group settings load once; their own changes after that are kept.

Prop firm mode

Your size is cut so that hitting your stop can't lose more than the room you have left today, counting trades you closed here and any open trade. Check your firm's dashboard for the real numbers.
